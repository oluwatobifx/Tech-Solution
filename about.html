About us

We help small businesses reach the world

TechSolutions exists so that a growing business can look, sell and trade like a big one — without the big-company budget.

Our story

Great products deserve a bigger market

Across Africa, millions of small businesses make products the world wants — but most still sell only to the people who walk past their shop. Going online is confusing, advertising feels risky, and finding trustworthy partners abroad is hard.

TechSolutions brings it together: an online store, marketing that works, clear sales numbers and a network of importers and exporters — so you can reach the wider world in a short time, and make your business simple for customers to use.

  • Built for small and growing businesses
  • Local payments and international trade
  • Real support from real people
  • Clear prices, no surprises
