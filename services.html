FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need technical skills?

No. We set everything up with you, and the dashboards are designed for business owners, not developers.

How is this different from Shopify?

You get an online store like Shopify, plus marketing management, transaction tracking and a B2B network for import and export partnerships — in one place.

Can I sell to customers outside my country?

Yes. You can accept international payments, list your business for importers abroad and send B2B invoices in other currencies.

How do partnership requests work?

Find a business in the network, send a request describing your offer, and they reply directly to your email.

Can I keep my existing website?

Yes. We can connect your current website and domain, or build a new one for you.
